Editor’s Letter

Six Months In, and Already My Favourite Story

The editor, photographed by the other half.

Every issue starts with a blank page, and so did we. Six months ago I had no idea that one ordinary Tuesday would quietly become the first page of the best thing that ever happened to me.

Since then we’ve collected a ridiculous amount of material: 5pm conversations, terrible jokes that only we understand, rainy days, Brew sandwiches, arguments about nothing and the kind of quiet moments that feel like home.

This issue is my attempt to put all of it in one place. It’s part scrapbook, part love letter, part proof that you are, without question, the best-dressed, funniest and kindest person I know (with the best dimples, obviously).

Inside you’ll find our story told twice (both times by me, obviously), a timeline of every first, a contract you’ve already signed, a crossword only you can solve, and a few secrets we probably shouldn’t have printed.

Turn the pages slowly. Play the games. Laugh at us. And know that every word in here means the same thing: I would choose you again, every single time.

With all my love,

The Cover Story

Months
of Us

Two people, one very long conversation, and a love that only gets better with time. Inside the romance of the year, and it’s only getting started.

By · Photographed by our phones

It started, as the best things do, without either of us noticing. A conversation that went on a little too long. A laugh that came a little too easily. Then, somewhere between the first hello and the hundredth goodnight, it became obvious: this was it.

“Somewhere between the first hello and the hundredth goodnight, it became obvious.”

Six months later, the conversation hasn’t stopped. It has moved through two seasons, through bad days and the very best ones. We’ve learned each other’s food orders, silences and secret playlists. And still, every evening at 5pm, there’s something new to talk about.

That, it turns out, is the whole secret. Not grand gestures (although those are nice too) but choosing each other in the tiny moments, over and over, until six months feels like six minutes.

Our Love Story

Part I · As told by

From the very beginning, there was something I couldn’t quite explain. Being around you felt easy, warm and completely new, like finding a song I already knew all the words to.

I remember the little things most: the first time you laughed at one of my terrible jokes, the way you said my name, how quickly “see you later” turned into “see you tonight”.

You taught me patience, laughter and the beauty of growing together. You make ordinary days feel like something worth writing down.

Today, I choose you with a full heart, grateful for a love that feels gentle, steady and true, and excited for every chapter we haven’t written yet.

Part II · As told by

“Some people arrive quietly and end up changing everything.”

You brought warmth and meaning into moments I never noticed before. With you, love became something deeper than words, built on understanding, respect and endless support.

You make the boring parts exciting and the hard parts bearable. You are my favourite person to do nothing with.

Today, I choose you, proud to walk beside the person who makes my world feel complete.
